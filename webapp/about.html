Your books, closed properly — every month.

Hazina is bookkeeping for small and micro traders in Kenya. M-Pesa statements and paper receipts go in; reconciled books and a KRA filing pack come out — with Hazi, the agent, doing the month-end beside you. This page explains how it works, where your data goes, and how you sign in.

What Hazina does

Reads your M-Pesa statement

Upload the statement file, paste the text, or photograph it. Every line lands in your month's ledger, in the order it happened.

Reads your receipts

Photograph a paper receipt and Hazina reads the vendor, date and amount. Nothing counts until you confirm the reading — the figures are always yours to correct.

Reconciles the month

Receipts are matched to statement lines, business money is separated from personal money, and anything unclear becomes a question for you — never a guess.

Builds your filing pack

Your tax position is computed from the current KRA rules — Turnover Tax where it applies — and saved as a pack you file from. Hazina prepares the pack; the filing itself stays with you.

Ask Hazi

Ask about your own month in plain words — what came in, what a figure is made of, what is still missing — and Hazi answers from your record, quoting your figures exactly.

Locks the month

A closed month is locked with its pack and a full activity record, so what was filed can always be shown again, exactly as it was.

How a month works

  1. Send the statementFile, pasted text, or a photo of your M-Pesa statement for the month.
  2. Add the receiptsPhotograph paper receipts as you go, or add them all at month-end.
  3. Hazi drafts the closeLines are sorted, receipts matched, and anything unclear is asked as a question.
  4. You confirmAnswer the questions and confirm the readings. You approve the month — Hazi does not close it alone.
  5. Close and keep the packThe month locks and the filing pack is saved with it, ready for your KRA filing.

Where your data goes

Your books are your business's private record. Here is exactly where each part of them goes:

  • Your records stay in your workspace. Statement lines, receipts' details, months and filing packs are stored in our database in the European Union, inside your business's own workspace.
  • Photos are read once and never kept. When you photograph a receipt or a statement, the picture is read to extract the figures, and only the figures are saved — together with a fingerprint of the file. The image itself is not stored.
  • A model does the reading; our code does the books. Receipt and statement photos, and your questions to Hazi, are processed by a model service (Meta's). Every figure in your books and your tax pack is computed by Hazina's own code, from the record you confirmed.
  • Only your workspace opens your books. You sign in with Google — Google verifies your email — or with your business's access code. When a workspace is created, a recovery code is shown once; only a fingerprint (a hash) of it is stored, never the code itself. Do not share your codes.
  • Encrypted on the way and at rest. Everything travels over HTTPS, and the database provider encrypts your records where they are stored.
  • We never sell your books, and never share them. Your records are yours. Every change in your workspace is written to an activity trail that you can see, so what happened to your books is always on record.
  • Your rights under Kenya's Data Protection Act. You can ask us for a copy of your business's data, or ask for it to be deleted — write to hello@ansaitechnologies.co.ke.
  • Nothing is sent to KRA from Hazina. Hazina prepares your filing pack; you file with KRA yourself, on iTax. Your figures leave Hazina only when you take them there.
